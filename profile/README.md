## 🏛️ About Loo La La Toiletique
**Loo La La Toiletique** is an international luxury sanitation organization headquartered in Istanbul, Türkiye. We do not operate simple restrooms; we design and maintain **Sanctuaries**—private, soundproof, climate-controlled, theme-based architectural retreats engineered for unhurried comfort, hygiene etiquette, and total sensory relaxation.
Our global hubs feature interconnected meeting halls, social intersections, refreshment lounges, and 24/7 automated access points, delivering a sanctuary experience tailored to every member.

## 🎟️ Onboarding, Biometrics & Operations
* **Application Process:** Applicants complete an intake questionnaire (including referee details and past 1-ply trauma history). Standard application fees are converted **100% into spendable deposit credits** within the member's account.
* **LooDatabase Biometrics:** Facility access across all global hubs is governed via an eye-profile laser scan saved directly to the **LooDatabase**. Legacy physical membership cards displaying digital avatars/PFPs are issued alongside biometric enrollment.
* **Guest Privileges:** First-year members receive **3 Guest Passes** annually. Host members must accompany their guests inside hub commons and intersections.
* **Currency (Loo Points):** Members earn **Loo Points** (or *Plush Points*) through daily check-ins, sanctuary visits, writing detailed reviews, and referring new members. Points can be redeemed for extra guest passes, limited-time roll colors, and LooStore items.
